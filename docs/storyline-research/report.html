Short answer

What I found and what I recommend

Yes, converting everything to HTML5 is practical. The 325 live Storyline units come from 269 source projects, which reduce to about 187 distinct activities, because many units reuse the same project. Those activities follow a handful of repeating templates, and about a dozen reusable engines would cover all of them.

Estimated effort: about 45–55 working days of AI-assisted build. That includes the engines plus pulling each activity's content out of Storyline and checking it. The first wave alone covers 65% of live units.

The good news

  • The content can mostly be extracted automatically (about 75–90% per family). The Marathi text is real Unicode (Mangal font in 267 of 269 projects). Answer keys sit in predictable places: trigger conditions, shape states and drag-drop pairs.
  • Most activities are the same template copied per letter or per topic, so one engine pays off many times over.
  • The crossword engine you already have proves the pipeline from engine to data to SCORM 1.2 package. Its shared code (lms.js, fit.js, package.py) carries straight over.

What needs your attention

  • Rights: most art and many voices were made by the vendors (Newgen/Exult, and TreeTo in 2020). Some images come from your stock folders, and a few files trace back to another client's project. See assets & rights.
  • Live errors: about 10 units show the wrong letter, have broken audio, or have a game that can be beaten without learning. See live problems.
  • Pedagogy: I propose some simplifications. One example: the 5 decorative "teaser" gimmicks become one card screen. These need your approval.

Deepti's TalentLMS units (light review)

Structural only: TalentLMS doesn't expose unit content through the API

What's there

  • 582 Assignment and 239 Content units, almost all in Level 2 (158 Content) and Level 1 (68). 142 of the Content units are answer sheets.
  • 167 live courses in Levels 0–4 have no Storyline at all: 107 are lesson-led and 60 are assignment-only.
  • The standard shape is a mini-lesson, then Assignment n, then "Answers for Assignment n". Course shells were bulk-created on 19–22 Sep 2020 and mostly filled in during 2021. Level 3 stalled, with 5 of its 7 courses empty.

Quick wins

  • Every course requires all units, and none has prerequisites, so answer sheets can be opened early unless sequential order is on. Consider hiding them until submission.
  • 14 test or placeholder courses are visible in the catalogue, and 11 live courses are empty shells. Archive or hide them.
  • Fix the mislabels: course 342 (अः) holds "Assignment 2 for अ (clone)"; 476's Visarg lesson is titled "Anuswar"; 808 has its assignment before the Storyline.
  • Later: the 3 Level 2 "Simple Crossword" courses can feed the existing crossword engine. A conjunct-builder engine (about 1.5 days) could serve the ~31 Jodakshare courses.

How it was done

  • Every .story was read as a zip in memory; nothing was extracted next to the source, and nothing on the B: drive was written.
  • TalentLMS was read with GET calls only (courses, units, categories). One unit page fetch returned the login page, which confirmed that unit content can't be read this way.
  • Matching used player titles stored in each project, file and folder names, letter decoding (L1-1NN codes, module numbering), semester and week, and number ranges.
  • 8 AI agents deep-dived the families using slide preview sheets drawn from the XML.
